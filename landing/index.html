What it does while you do something else

The popup is only the part you see. The real work happens in a background worker that talks straight to Twitch.

Every 5 minutes
It asks Twitch who is live among your channels, up to 100 of them per request.
Every minute
While someone on your list is live, checks speed up so the next one doesn't slip by.
One click
Click the notification or its Watch button to open the stream, or pause the streamer until tomorrow right from it.

02

The rest of the popup

Small things that make a long list easier to live with.

team/karminecorp

Whole teams

Type team/ followed by a Twitch team name to add every member at once, or import the channels you already follow on Twitch from the settings.

1 h / tomorrow

Snooze one channel

Mute a streamer for an hour, until tomorrow morning or for good, without removing them. A whole team can be muted from the settings.

next: tomorrow, 8:00 PM

Next scheduled stream

Offline channels show their next stream when it's set in their Twitch schedule.

8 712 viewers

Live preview

Unfold a live streamer to see the thumbnail, how long they have been live and the exact viewer count.

auto / dark / light

Light or dark

Follows the Chrome theme by default, or pick one in the settings.

backup.json

History, stats and backup

Every live Nowtify catches is logged with its duration, and the settings page sums it up. Export everything to a file to move to another browser.

Nowtify server doesn't exist

Your list, history and settings are stored by Chrome on your machine. The only requests go to Twitch, to log in and to ask who is live. No analytics, no tracker, and even the fonts on this page are served from this site.

04

Questions people ask

Why does it need my Twitch account?

Twitch refuses API requests without a token, even for public data. By default Nowtify asks for no permission on your account: the token can only read what anyone can already see on Twitch. The only exception is the optional follows import, which asks to read the list of channels you follow.

How long before the notification shows up?

Nowtify checks every 5 minutes, and every minute once someone on your list is live. Expect the notification within 5 minutes of the stream starting.

Does it work when Chrome is closed?

No, Chrome has to be open. A minimized window is enough.

Firefox or Safari?

Not for now. Edge, Brave and Vivaldi can install it straight from the Chrome Web Store.

Is it really free?

Yes, and the code is on GitHub under the MIT license. If you want to chip in, there's a Ko-fi.
